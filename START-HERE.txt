STELLARIS BIGSTACK FIX — START HERE

For Macs with Stellaris installed through Steam (macOS 11 or later).
Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. No programming tools needed.

1. CHOOSE YOUR MODS
Open Stellaris as usual. Choose your playset in the Paradox Launcher.
Then quit the game and launcher.

2. OPEN TERMINAL
Press Command-Space, type Terminal, and press Return.
Terminal is already on your Mac.

3. RUN SETUP
Type the word bash and then one space in Terminal.
Drag INSTALL.command from this folder into the Terminal window.
Click the Terminal window and press Return.

Setup copies the fix into your game and copies a Steam setting for you.
If it asks where the game is, open Steam, right-click Stellaris, and choose
Manage > Browse local files. Select that folder in the setup window.

4. PASTE INTO STEAM
In your Steam Library, right-click Stellaris and choose Properties.
Under General, find Launch Options. Clear anything already in that box.
Press Command-V to paste the setting that setup copied.

5. PLAY
Close Properties and click Play. The game opens directly, using the mods
you chose in step 1. It skips the Paradox Launcher.
You can close Terminal and delete this downloaded folder and ZIP now.

TO CHANGE MODS
Clear Launch Options in Steam. Click Play, choose your playset in the
Paradox Launcher, then quit. Run setup again and paste the setting back.
Download the ZIP again if you deleted it.

TO UNDO THE FIX
Clear Launch Options in Steam. The game will start normally again.
